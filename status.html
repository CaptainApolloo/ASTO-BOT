Connection
Press this key to reconnect to ASTO-BOT immediately — useful right after starting the app, instead of waiting for the next automatic retry. While the connection is up, pressing it sends a ping to confirm it works.

Like every ASTO-BOT key, it lights up while ASTO-BOT is reachable and turns grey when it is not.

Turn the server on in ASTO-BOT under Settings → Websocket and click Save & Apply.
